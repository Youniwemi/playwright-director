Your first invoice

Send your first invoice in a minute. By the end, your client receives a numbered invoice by email, and you can follow its payment from this list.

Read the documentation ↗

Why drafts have no number. Invoice numbers must follow each other with no gap, so a number is only given when the invoice is sent.

Sending is final. Once sent, an invoice can no longer be edited, only cancelled with a credit note.
